Data sources & availability

Timing, entries, classes, sectors and Race Control: the public Swiss Timing / SRO feed. Push revision notifications trigger fresh HTTPS documents, streamed to this page immediately. HTTP polling every 5 seconds remains as fallback. A completed session stays labelled as completed.

Current race follows the official weekend schedule across series. Select GT3 · Verstappen #3 to pin that race. Practice and qualifying are excluded. The feed provides S1/S2/S3 times and sector-based progress, not F1 mini-sectors. Map positions are estimated from sector progress, not GPS. Team radio, tyre age and track weather are unavailable. Lap history, pace and pit events accumulate while the local server is running.
